FAQ

What is TrialTracker?

TrialTracker is a simple dashboard that helps you keep track of free trials and recurring subscriptions, so you know what's about to renew and don't get surprised by a charge.

Is it free?

Yes, TrialTracker is free to use.

Can I track normal subscriptions as well as free trials?

Yes. When adding an item you choose either Trial (a one-time end date) or Subscription (recurring monthly or yearly). Subscriptions automatically roll forward to their next renewal date once the current one passes.

When are reminders sent?

You'll get an email 7 days before, and again 1 day before, a trial ends or a subscription renews. Each reminder is only sent once per billing cycle.

Does TrialTracker cancel subscriptions for me?

No. TrialTracker only tracks dates and sends reminders — it doesn't connect to any vendor account, and it can't cancel or modify a trial or subscription on your behalf. You stay in control of that.

What does "Potential Monthly Spend" mean?

It's the total monthly cost across everything on your dashboard if every trial converted and every subscription stayed active. Yearly subscriptions are converted to a monthly-equivalent (yearly cost ÷ 12) for this total so it's a fair comparison across billing cycles.

Is my information private?

Yes. Each account only ever sees its own trials and subscriptions. See the Privacy Policy for details on what's collected and why.

Can I edit or delete a trial or subscription?

Yes — use Edit or Delete on any card on your dashboard at any time.

How do I contact TrialTracker or request deletion of my account data?

See the Contact page.
